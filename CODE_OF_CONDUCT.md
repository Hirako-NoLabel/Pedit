# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome contributors with different experience, backgrounds and communication styles. Discuss technical choices on their merits. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized or abusive language.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces where people represent the project. Respect document confidentiality and the rights of authors and asset creators.

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository maintainer's published contact channel. Before public launch, maintainers must publish a monitored private contact method; none is invented in this snapshot. If none is available, request a private contact channel in an issue without naming affected people or posting incident details. Security defects follow SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a correction, warn, temporarily restrict or ban participation proportionately. They should protect reporters' privacy, explain decisions when safe, consider appeals and recuse themselves from conflicts of interest.

This policy was written for Pedit and is covered by the project license.
